Housing & Accommodations
Caprock offers modern, comfortable accommodations designed specifically for extended stays and workforce living. Thoughtfully designed spaces support both rest and productivity, creating an environment where residents can settle in, recharge, and feel at home throughout their assignment.
Housing features include:
•	Private Single Occupancy Accommodations
•	Fully Furnished Rooms
•	Wi-Fi Access
•	Laundry Facilities
•	Climate-controlled Living Spaces
•	Housekeeping Services
•	Shared Social Spaces
Bringing Your RV?
Prefer to bring your home on the road with you? Caprock also offers RV accommodations for residents looking for additional flexibility during their stay.
The on-site RV park provides convenient access to dining, recreation, wellness, and resident services, allowing guests to enjoy the full Caprock experience while traveling with their RV.
